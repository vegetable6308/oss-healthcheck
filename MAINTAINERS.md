## Primary maintainer

## Responsibilities

Maintainers triage issues, review pull requests, keep CI and dependencies healthy, publish releases, respond to private vulnerability reports, and keep roadmap decisions public.

The maintainer roster will be updated when contributors earn reviewer or core-maintainer status under GOVERNANCE.md. Git history and GitHub activity provide the public record of ongoing maintenance.
